Oceans cover more than 70 percent of Earth's surface and host diverse ecosystems. Coral reefs are among the most biodiverse environments on the planet. The open ocean or pelagic zone is home to fish, whales, and plankton. Deep sea ecosystems survive without sunlight using chemosynthesis. Oceans regulate climate by absorbing heat and CO2. Overfishing, pollution, and warming threaten marine biodiversity globally.
