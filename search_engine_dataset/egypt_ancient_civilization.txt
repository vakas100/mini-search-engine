Ancient Egypt was one of the world's earliest and greatest civilizations. It developed along the Nile River around 3100 BC. The pharaohs were considered divine rulers and oversaw massive construction projects. The pyramids of Giza were built as royal tombs around 2500 BC. Ancient Egyptians developed hieroglyphics, papyrus writing, and advanced medicine. The civilization flourished for over 3000 years before being conquered by Rome.
