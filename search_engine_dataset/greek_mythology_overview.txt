Greek mythology is a collection of myths and legends from ancient Greece. The Olympian gods led by Zeus lived on Mount Olympus. Major deities include Athena, Apollo, Poseidon, and Aphrodite. Heroes like Hercules, Achilles, and Odysseus feature prominently in myths. Stories were passed down through oral tradition and later written texts like the Iliad and the Odyssey. Greek myths influenced Western literature and art.
